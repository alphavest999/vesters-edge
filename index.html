Vesters Edge Real Estate Development and Management Company

We develop and manage real estate with careful planning up front and consistent management after completion, so properties hold their value over the long term.

What we do

Real estate development

Site evaluation, feasibility, planning and project oversight from early concept through completion.

Property management

Scheduled maintenance, responsive tenant service, financial reporting and record keeping.

Asset planning

Reserve planning and long-term strategy to protect income and property value.

Our approach

  1. Answer the hard questions early. Zoning, site conditions, market demand, contingency and schedule are settled on paper before construction begins
  2. Design for operations. How a property will be run shapes how it is built
  3. Manage consistently. Maintenance on a schedule, numbers reviewed monthly, and reserves built before they are needed
  4. Document everything. Complete records support financing, insurance and an eventual sale
